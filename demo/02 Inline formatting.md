# Inline formatting

**Bold**, *italic*, ***bold italic***, ~~strikethrough~~ and `inline code`.

Marks can combine: **bold with *italic* inside**, ~~struck **bold**~~, and `code` next to **bold**.

Underscores work too: _italic_ and __bold__ (Monocle saves them as `*` and `**` when the line is edited).

Escaped characters stay literal: \*not italic\*, \# not a heading, \[not a link\].

Arrows and comparisons are written as typed: a -> b, x < y, y > z.
